Chess pieces: the cburnett set (site/assets/pieces/cburnett.svg)

The 12 drawings in cburnett.svg are Chess_{k,q,r,b,n,p}{l,d}t45.svg from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces), each created on 2006-12-27 by the
Commons user Cburnett (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett), who offers them under
several licences, a BSD licence among them. Blink elects the BSD 3-Clause License option below and
relies on no other licence for these files.

Changes: the 12 files are packed into one SVG sprite, one <g id="wk"> .. <g id="bp"> group per piece,
with inner element ids removed. The drawings themselves are unchanged. The sprite is rebuilt with
`blink site pieces --src <directory holding the 12 Commons files>`.
